A cumulative review built from the foundational NUR 325 Pathopharmacology decks — pharmacology principles, cellular adaptation, infection & immunity, cardiovascular, endocrine/metabolic, hematology, fluid & electrolytes, neuro, psych, and GI/GU/skin. Ten topic pages plus a build-your-own NCLEX-style practice exam.

Fluid & Electrolytes

Osmosis/oncotic pressure, sodium/potassium/calcium/magnesium/phosphate imbalances and treatment.

Pain & Seizures

 9

Dementia, Depression & SUD

Delirium vs. dementia, Alzheimer's meds, antidepressants/anxiolytics, withdrawal syndromes.

 10

GI, GU, Skin & Hypersensitivity

Laxatives/antidiarrheals, UTIs/OAB, hypersensitivity types & EpiPen, antifungals, skin disorders, steroids.
